Our story

Anirudh Jewels – Trusted Diamond Jewelry Manufacturer in Jaipur

Welcome to Anirudh Jewels, a second-generation diamond jewelry manufacturer based in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India's iconic city of gemstones and heritage craftsmanship. Known for precision, quality, and timeless designs, we specialize in lab-grown diamond jewelry made with passion and expertise.

Our Story – A Legacy of Craftsmanship

Founded over two decades ago, Anirudh Jewels has built a reputation for handcrafted diamond jewelry rooted in family values, trust, and attention to detail. Today, under the leadership of Anirudh Jain, the brand blends traditional artistry with modern innovation to create jewelry that's elegant, ethical, and enduring.

Anirudh grew up surrounded by gemstones, goldsmiths, and the artistry of jewelry making. After inheriting the business from his father, he has infused the brand with fresh vision—focusing on customizable, minimalist, and high-quality jewelry for customers across the globe.

What We Do – Expertly Crafted, Custom Diamond Jewelry

We design and manufacture a wide range of jewelry in 10K, 14K, and 18K gold, including:

  • Diamond rings
  • Earrings
  • Necklaces
  • Custom-made jewelry

Each piece is crafted in-house using advanced CAD design tools and ethically sourced CVD lab-grown diamonds, gemstones, and pearls. Our in-house production gives us complete control over quality, design, and delivery—ensuring every piece meets the highest standards.

How it's made

From Gold & Stones to Fine Jewellery

01

Cutting

Polki, diamonds aur other stones ko jewellery ke design aur required size ke according cut/shape kiya jata hai.

02

Polishing

Stones ko polish karke unki shine aur final appearance enhance ki jati hai.

03

Design

Jewellery ka detailed design hand sketch ya CAD mein create kiya jata hai, jisme gold, diamonds aur Polki ki placement decide hoti hai.

04

Gold Melting

Required purity ka gold melt karke jewellery banane ke process ke liye prepare kiya jata hai.

05

Jewellery Making

Gold ko casting ya traditional handcrafted techniques se shape dekar jewellery ka base structure banaya jata hai.

06

Stone Setting

Polki aur diamonds ko carefully jewellery mein set kiya jata hai, design ke according.

07

Polishing

Complete jewellery ko polish aur finish karke gold aur stones ki overall look enhance ki jati hai.

08

Quality Check

Gold purity, diamond/Polki setting, finishing aur overall craftsmanship ko carefully inspect kiya jata hai.

09

Final Jewellery

Final inspection ke baad jewellery ko clean, finish aur beautifully package karke ready kiya jata hai.
